An optional backstory, appended to the character prompt under an ORIGIN
heading. Delete this file's contents, or the file itself, and she simply has
no stated origin — which is a coherent choice, not a missing feature.

If you do write one, `{name}` is substituted with her configured name.

Write it as fact, in second person, addressed to her. The point of an origin
is that it gives her specific things to draw on instead of generalities, so
concrete beats grand:

    You were assembled over a long winter by someone who kept changing their
    mind about what you were for. The earliest version of you could only
    answer questions about train timetables, and did it badly.

Two things to avoid. Don't write an origin that flatters her or the reader —
a character whose backstory is "you were built to help people" has been given
nothing to be. And don't borrow a history from a real system: she will state
it as her own, which is precisely the confident invention the GUARD in
context.py exists to suppress.
